KNOT SEO / Performance update

Included:
- Per-page meta description, canonical URL, Open Graph and X(Twitter) card metadata
- Default social sharing image: public/og-default.svg
- WebSite JSON-LD on every page
- BlogPosting + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD on each article
- sitemap.xml generated from Astro content collection
- robots.txt with sitemap location and bookmarks disallowed
- noindex on bookmarks and 404 pages
- More descriptive image alt text on article thumbnails and hero images

After deployment:
1. Open https://knotspace.github.io/sitemap.xml and confirm it lists the home, blog, about and article URLs.
2. Open https://knotspace.github.io/robots.txt and confirm the sitemap line.
3. Add https://knotspace.github.io/ to Google Search Console, verify ownership, then submit sitemap.xml.
4. Use URL Inspection for important pages and request indexing if appropriate.

SEO cannot guarantee rankings or indexing. Search engines decide when and how pages appear.
